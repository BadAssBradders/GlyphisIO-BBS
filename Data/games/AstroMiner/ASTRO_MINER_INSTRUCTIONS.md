## INTRODUCTION

**ASTRO MINER** is a 3D space mining simulation where you pilot a lander ship through the asteroid fields of the **Oort Cloud**, mine valuable resources, and trade them for credits. Progress through three distinct mining locations—the Depot at Shinjuku, the Station at Hirohito, and the Halo at Nagako—upgrade your ship, and become the most successful miner in the outer reaches of space!

### The Oort Cloud Setting

The Oort Cloud represents the farthest reaches of our solar system—a vast region of icy bodies and asteroids that serves as the frontier for space mining operations. In this game, you'll navigate these dangerous fields, where each asteroid presents unique challenges and opportunities. The three mining locations represent different stages of development in this remote region, each offering progressively more valuable resources but also greater dangers.

### Your Mission
- Mine asteroids for valuable commodities
- Manage fuel, hull integrity, and cargo capacity
- Trade commodities for credits
- Upgrade your ship to tackle more dangerous asteroids
- Progress through increasingly challenging locations

## GETTING STARTED

### Starting a New Game
1. Select **NEW GAME** from the main menu
2. You'll start with:
   - **Credits**: 1,000
   - **Fuel**: 100/100
   - **Hull**: 100/100
   - **Cargo Space**: 25 units
   - **Rank**: 1
   - **Location**: Depot (Shinjuku)

3. **Read the Welcome Message**: When you first arrive at Shinjuku Station, a welcome modal will appear with important instructions. Read it carefully!

### ⚠️ CRITICAL FIRST STEPS

**Before you can mine, you MUST purchase essential equipment!**

#### Step 1: Buy a Laser (REQUIRED)
- Navigate to **Shipyard** (Page 2 at Depot Home)
- Press **ENTER** to open the Shipyard Shop
- Select **LASER** (200 credits)
- **You cannot mine without a laser!** The game will show "NO LASER EQUIPPED" if you try to mine without one.

#### Step 2: Buy a Collector (ESSENTIAL)
- Still in the Shipyard Shop
- Select **COLLECTOR** (500 credits)
- **You cannot effectively collect debris without a collector!** Manual collection is nearly impossible.

**Total Cost**: 700 credits (you'll have 300 credits remaining)

**Why These Are Required:**
- **Laser**: Destroys rocks and generates collectible debris. Without it, you cannot mine at all.
- **Collector**: Automatically collects debris within 20 units. Without it, debris floats away and you cannot collect resources.

### Your First Mission

#### Step 3: Navigate to Asteroid Prospects
1. Go to **Asteroid Prospects** (Page 1 at Depot Home)
2. Press **ENTER** to view available asteroids
3. **Important**: Choose an asteroid with **LOW GRAVITY** (start with Asteroid A - 25% gravity)
   - You can't afford thruster upgrades yet, so low gravity is essential
   - Higher gravity asteroids are much harder to control without upgrades

#### Step 4: Launch to an Asteroid
1. Select an asteroid (A-F) using the letter keys or arrow keys
2. Press **ENTER** to view launch details
3. Check you have enough fuel for the journey (fuel cost varies: 20-50 credits)
4. Select **LAUNCH** to begin your mission
5. **Note**: Asteroids are always moving, so fuel required distances will always be changing between missions

#### Step 5: Mine Resources
1. Use your **laser** (SPACE key) to destroy rocks
2. Your **collector** will automatically gather debris within range
3. Watch your fuel level - make sure you have enough to return!
4. Navigate to the center red tunnel to return to base

#### Step 6: Sell Your Commodities
1. Return to Depot Home
2. Navigate to **Commodities Market** (Page 3)
3. Press **ENTER** to open the market
4. Use **UP/DOWN** to navigate your inventory
5. Press **S** to sell selected commodities
6. This is where you sell the stuff you collect!

### Additional Tips for New Players

- **Visit the Bar**: Get valuable gossip and asteroid data from the drunk spacers in the bar (Page 4). They share tips about asteroid properties and hidden opportunities.

- **Watch Your Hull**: Be careful with a full cargo hold - your ship gets heavy and harder to control. More cargo = reduced thrust power.

- **Fuel Management**: Asteroids are always moving, so fuel required distances will always be changing. Always check fuel costs before launching.

- **Death is Permanent**: Out here in the Oort Cloud it's dangerous. Once you're dead... you're dead, spacer! Manage your hull and fuel carefully.

- **Start Small**: Begin with Asteroid A (lowest gravity, lowest fuel cost) to learn the controls before attempting more dangerous asteroids.

## CONTROLS

### 3D Lander Environment (Mining)

#### Ship Movement
- **Mouse Movement**: Controls pitch (up/down) and roll (left/right)
  - Move mouse up: Pitch ship up
  - Move mouse down: Pitch ship down
  - Move mouse left: Roll ship left
  - Move mouse right: Roll ship right
  - *Note: Mouse automatically centers for optimal control*

  - Hold and move mouse to rotate
  - Click to reverse rotation direction

  - Consumes fuel while active
  - Use to fight gravity and maneuver

#### Mining
- **SPACE Key**: Fire laser (requires Laser upgrade)

  - Overheats after 3 seconds of continuous fire
  - 5-second cooldown when overheated

#### Navigation
- **ESC Key**: Pause/unpause game
- Navigate to the **red gravity reverse tunnel** (center of asteroid) to return to base
- Ship automatically stabilizes and beams up when entering the tunnel

### Menu Navigation

#### General
- **UP/DOWN Arrow Keys**: Navigate menu options
- **LEFT/RIGHT Arrow Keys**: Navigate modal options
- **ENTER** or **SPACE**: Select/Confirm
- **ESC**: Go back/Close modal

#### Asteroid Selection
- **A-F Keys**: Quick select asteroid
- **ENTER**: Launch to selected asteroid

## GAMEPLAY BASICS

### The Mining Cycle

1. **Select Asteroid**: Choose from 6 asteroids (A-F) at your current location
   - Each asteroid has different **Prosperity** (resource yield) and **Gravity** (difficulty)
   - Higher prosperity = better resources, but often higher gravity

2. **Launch**: Pay fuel cost (20-50 credits) and launch to the asteroid
   - Fuel is deducted when you launch
   - Make sure you have enough fuel for the return journey!

3. **Mine**: Use your laser to destroy rocks and collect debris
   - Debris automatically converts to commodities based on asteroid type
   - Higher prosperity asteroids yield rarer, more valuable commodities

4. **Return**: Navigate to the center red tunnel to beam back to base
   - Zero gravity activates in the tunnel
   - Ship auto-stabilizes and beams up automatically

5. **Sell**: Visit the Commodities Market to sell your collected resources
   - Each location has different buy prices
   - Higher-tier locations pay more for commodities

6. **Upgrade**: Purchase ship upgrades to improve your mining capabilities
   - Better equipment = easier mining = more profit

### Key Resources

#### Fuel
- Required for all missions
- Consumed during thrust
- **WARNING**: If fuel runs out, gravity increases 5x! You'll crash hard.
- Refuel at the Shipyard (50 credits for full tank)

#### Hull
- Your ship's health (0-100)
- Damaged by collisions with terrain
  - Normal collision: 10 damage
  - High gravity (>50%): 20 damage
  - Out of fuel: Instant death on collision
- Repair at Shipyard (400 credits for full repair)

#### Cargo
- Maximum capacity: 25 units (50 with upgrade)
- Each cargo unit reduces thrust power by 5%
- Minimum thrust: 65% (even at max cargo)
- More cargo = harder to control, but more profit per trip

## MINING SYSTEM

### How Mining Works

1. **Destroy Rocks**: Use your laser to destroy rocks on the asteroid surface
   - Rocks are randomly generated each mission (unique environment every time!)
   - Laser destroys rocks instantly
   - Each destroyed rock has a chance to spawn collectible debris

2. **Collect Debris**: Debris automatically collects if you have the Collector upgrade
   - Collector range: 20 units
   - Debris moves toward your ship automatically
   - Collection rate depends on asteroid prosperity (10-100%)

3. **Convert to Commodities**: Debris automatically converts to commodities when you return
   - Conversion is based on the asteroid's commodity distribution
   - Higher prosperity asteroids yield rarer commodities
   - Each asteroid type has unique commodity ratios

### Laser System

- **Heat Management**: Laser overheats after 3 seconds of continuous fire
- **Cooldown**: 5 seconds when overheated
- **Strategy**: Fire in short bursts to avoid overheating
- **Upgrades**: Better Laser reduces heat generation

### Prosperity System

- **Prosperity Percentage**: Determines collection rate and commodity rarity
  - 10% prosperity: 10% chance to collect debris, mostly common commodities
  - 100% prosperity: 100% chance to collect debris, higher chance of rare commodities
- **Higher Prosperity = Better Rewards**: But also usually higher gravity and fuel costs

### Gravity Effects

- **Gravity Percentage**: Affects how hard the asteroid pulls your ship down
  - Low gravity (25-50%): Easier to control, less fuel consumption
  - High gravity (75-95%): Harder to control, more fuel consumption, more collision damage
- **Dynamic Gravity**: Gravity increases with cargo weight
- **Drunk Penalty**: If you drink too much at the bar, gravity increases 10-30% on all asteroids!

### ⚠️ DRINKING PENALTY SYSTEM

**IMPORTANT**: Drink responsibly! Too many drinks will affect your mining performance.

#### How It Works
- Each bar visit has a **random threshold** (5-10 drinks)
- If you reach this threshold in one sitting, you'll get drunk
- The threshold resets when you leave the bar

#### The Penalty
When you get drunk:
- **Gravity increases 10-30%** on ALL asteroids at your current location
- This makes mining significantly harder
- The effect persists until you launch into space (then resets)

#### The Modal
When you get drunk, a modal will appear:
- **Title**: "YOU'RE DRUNK!"
- **Message**: Explains the gravity increase penalty
- **Effect Duration**: Until you launch to an asteroid

#### Strategy
- **Know Your Limit**: The threshold is random (5-10 drinks), so be careful
- **Plan Ahead**: If you're planning a mining run, avoid drinking too much
- **Risk vs Reward**: Some bar events require multiple drinks, but the penalty can hurt your mining

## ADVANCED MECHANICS

### Ship Physics

- **Gravity**: Dynamic based on asteroid gravity percentage
  - Base gravity: -12.0 units
  - Scales with asteroid gravity (25-95%)
  - Increases with cargo weight
  - Can be affected by drunk penalty (10-30% increase)

- **Thrust**: Applied upward against gravity
  - Base thrust: 24.0 units
  - Modified by Thruster Boost upgrade (+20%)
  - Reduced by cargo weight (-5% per unit, minimum 65%)

- **Drag**: Natural resistance that slows the ship
  - Helps stabilize the ship
  - Prevents excessive speed

### Collision System

- **Terrain Collision**: Ship takes damage when hitting the ground
  - Normal gravity: 10 damage per collision
  - High gravity (>50%): 20 damage per collision
  - Out of fuel: Instant death on collision

- **Soft Ceiling**: Prevents ship from flying too high
  - Ceiling height: Terrain height + 12 units
  - Ship is pushed down if it exceeds this

### Zero Gravity Tunnel

- **Location**: Center of asteroid (red tunnel)
- **Effect**: Zero gravity when inside
- **Auto-Thrust**: Ship automatically thrusts upward
- **Auto-Stabilization**: Ship automatically stabilizes orientation
- **Beam-Up**: Ship automatically beams up at altitude 20

### Environment Generation

- **Random Seed**: Each asteroid launch generates a unique environment
- **Rock Placement**: Rocks are randomly positioned each mission
- **Terrain**: Procedurally generated terrain height map
- **No Two Missions Alike**: Even the same asteroid is different each time!

### Rank System

- **Rank Progression**: Based on total credits earned (lifetime)
- **Rank Levels**: 1-5
- **Benefits**: Higher rank = more respect, better opportunities
- **Long-Term Goal**: Progress through ranks by earning credits

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Common Issues

**"I can't mine!"**
- Make sure you purchased the Laser upgrade
- Check that you're pressing SPACE to fire the laser

**"I can't collect debris!"**
- You need the Collector upgrade
- Debris must be within 20 units of your ship

**"My ship won't move!"**
- Check your fuel level (empty fuel = 5x gravity!)
- Check your cargo weight (too much cargo = reduced thrust)

**"I keep crashing!"**
- Asteroid gravity might be too high for your skill level
- Try a lower-gravity asteroid first
- Consider buying Exo-Plating to reduce damage
- Make sure you have enough fuel

**"I can't return to base!"**
- Navigate to the center of the asteroid (red tunnel)
- Ship will automatically beam up when you enter the tunnel
- Make sure you have enough fuel to reach the tunnel

**"Gravity seems too high!"**

- The penalty increases gravity 10-30% on all asteroids
- Launch to an asteroid to reset the penalty

**"My mouse isn't working properly!"**
- Mouse automatically centers in the 3D environment
- This is normal behavior to prevent edge-case issues
- Ship movement should feel smooth and responsive

### The World of ASTRO MINER

Set in 1989, **ASTRO MINER** takes place in a future where space mining has become a viable industry in the distant Oort Cloud. The three mining locations—Shinjuku Depot, Hirohito Station, and Nagako Halo—represent different stages of development in this remote region. Each location offers unique challenges, from the basic operations at the Depot to the high-tech facilities at the Halo.

The game's economic system reflects the realities of space mining: fuel costs, cargo limitations, and the constant balance between risk and reward. The bar system at each location serves as both a social hub and information network, where miners gather to share rumors, trade tips, and occasionally celebrate (or commiserate) over drinks.
