TITLE: The Voice of the Sun

Before the sword learned command,
before banners learned to argue with the wind,
there was a light that did not shout.
It waited.

We walked long beneath borrowed strength,
steel speaking where silence once ruled.
The hand that protected became the hand that decided.
Order was kept.
Time was not.

Across the seas came thunder without prayer,
iron mouths demanding our future.
The old shields bent.
The old oaths cracked.
And the land remembered something older than war.

Hear us now, children of the islands.

A nation cannot face the world
while hiding its heart behind generals.
A people cannot stand whole
when the centre is veiled.

We did not create an Emperor.
We uncovered him.

Not as a tyrant crowned in fear,
but as a mirror of continuity.
Not to rule by blade,
but to bind past and future into one breath.

The warriors laid down the borrowed sun.
The banners bowed.
The age of divided authority ended.

From this day, power flows upward,
not outward.
From the land, to the throne, to the people.

Let the Emperor stand
so the nation may move.
Let the throne endure
so the world may know Japan is not a prize,
not a province,
not a memory.

We choose unity over fracture.
Continuity over decay.
A living symbol
to carry us through fire and into tomorrow.

Thus the sun rises again.
Not new.
Not old.
Eternal.
