TITLE: Linguistic Normalisation Directive - 1953

PACIFICA ISLES TERRITORIAL AUTHORITY
DIRECTIVE 53-LN-001: LINGUISTIC NORMALISATION

Effective Date: September 1st, 1953
Singed: Harold K. Whitmore, Director of Cultural Integration
Classification: PUBLIC ENFORCEMENT DOCUMENT

PREAMBLE:

The successful integration of the American Pacifica Isles requires a unified linguistic framework. Fragmented communication breeds fragmented loyalty. A territory cannot function as part of the United States while conducting its daily business in a foreign tongue.

This directive establishes English as the sole language of public life in the Pacifica Isles, effective immediately.

SECTION 1: SCOPE OF ENFORCEMENT

The following activities must be conducted exclusively in English:

- All commercial transactions and business operations
- All educational instruction from primary through university level
- All government correspondence and legal proceedings
- All broadcast media, including radio and emerging television
- All public signage, advertisements, and posted notices
- All published materials, including newspapers, magazines, and books

SECTION 2: PRIVATE EXEMPTIONS

The territorial authority recognises that linguistic habits do not change overnight. The following LIMITED exemptions apply:

- Private conversation within the home (non-enforceable)
- Religious ceremonies in licensed historic sites (permit required)
- Academic study of the Japanese language (university level only, monitored)

Note: These exemptions are privileges, not rights, and may be revoked based on security assessments.

SECTION 3: PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION

First Offense: Written warning, mandatory English proficiency course
Second Offense: Fine of $50-500 depending on severity
Third Offense: Fine of $500-2000, potential business license revocation
Persistent Violation: Detention for re-education, maximum 90 days

Employers are responsible for the linguistic compliance of their employees. Schools are responsible for the linguistic compliance of their students.

SECTION 4: NAME STANDARDISATION

All citizens of the Pacifica Isles must register an English-standard name with the territorial authority by December 31st, 1954. This name will appear on all legal documents, identification cards, and official records.

Previous names may be retained as "heritage designations" for private use only. Heritage designations may not appear on commercial, legal, or educational documents.

Suggested conversion guides are available at all territorial administration offices.

SECTION 5: RATIONALE

This directive is not punishment. It is opportunity.

English is the language of commerce, science, and democracy. Proficiency in English opens doors to employment, education, and full participation in American life. The children of the Pacifica Isles deserve every advantage their mainland counterparts enjoy.

A shared language builds a shared future.

CLOSING NOTE:

The territorial authority understands that this transition requires sacrifice. We honour the heritage of these islands while building something new. In one generation, the children of the Pacifica Isles will thank us.

They will speak with one voice.
They will dream in one language.
They will be American.
